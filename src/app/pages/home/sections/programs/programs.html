Our Programs

At V Care, we believe the first few years of a child's life shape everything that follows. Our preschool and activity centre in Karvenagar is built around one idea: children learn best when they feel safe, loved and free to be curious.

We're working to raise children who are confident, kind, and genuinely excited to learn — not through worksheets and pressure, but through play, stories, music and hands-on discovery, guided by teachers who know each child by name.

From a toddler's very first steps into a group setting, right through to structured after-school care, every V Care program is thoughtfully paced for its age group. Explore what's next for your little one.
